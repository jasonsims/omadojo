Is Super reaching the page?

Hold your Super key (⊞ Windows, ⌘ Mac). The SUPER light under the screen should glow. Then press Super + Return and a terminal should open in the fake desktop.

Your real OS often grabs Super combos first. On Omarchy, Hyprland will open a real terminal instead. Fix it with Lock keys: Chromium-based browsers go fullscreen and ask the compositor to pass shortcuts through (hold Esc to leave).
